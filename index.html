Welcome to Tripcast

Tripcast is a weather-aware activity planner. Enter a destination and date to get a forecast and activity recommendations.

How it works

  1. Go to Plan a Trip and enter a destination and date.
  2. Tripcast fetches real weather data for that location.
  3. You get activity suggestions based on the forecast conditions.
  4. Save plans to revisit them on the Saved Plans page.
